Frequently Asked Questions

Finewood is a fictional, adult (18+) serialized universe combining traditional Victorian academy discipline with modern Female-Led Marriage (FLM) domestic governance across 9 connected worlds (including Finewood, Nordlandia, and the Southern Manor). It is designed specifically for individuals who experience modern decision fatigue and desire the soothing, structured escapism of complete feminine authority and zero-choice routine.

Infractions follow a clear, realistic institutional progression established across our 537 scripts: Minor lapses begin with 15-minute Corner Time (hands on head) or 100-500 deportment lines. Dormitory and evening misbehavior is corrected via hairbrush inspection under Ms. Kathy. Serious defiance leads directly to Ms. Elaine's Discipline Liaison office for formal rattan cane correction, while persistent executive defiance is referred up through the 3-Tier Behavioral Counseling wing under Astra, Melanie, and Celeste.

Episodes are created using advanced AI text generation guided by careful human editing, paired with custom AI character art with strict visual continuity (Misha, Hawthorne, Eleanor, Beau, etc.), and professional-grade AI voice synthesis layered with custom sound design (leather ledgers, ticking grandfather clocks, rattan canes, and starched uniforms).

Student & Ward ranks (Cub $5, Senior Cub $10, Sissy-Maid $15, Head Cub $25) are designed for patrons seeking pure immersive entertainment, uncut serialized audio dramas, and soothing bedtime relief. Faculty tiers (Counselor $40, Instructor $60, Headmistress $80) unlock deep worldbuilding archives, prompt recipes, custom character dedications, and direct advisory input into upcoming sagas.
