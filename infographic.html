The 3-Tier Escalating Counseling Department

When a student exhibits rebellion or decision-making defiance, they are referred through escalating severity:

Severity Level I Remedial

Red Victorian dress, brunette hair.

Initial psychological intake. Explores where the student lost their willingness to obey and establishes baseline routines.

Severity Level II Intensive

Melanie

Brown Victorian dress, chignon, brown glasses.

Deep behavioral boundary enforcement. Reluctance is met with strict behavioral exercises and demerit notices.

Severity Level III Final / Austere

Celeste

Austere black Victorian dress, raven hair.

The final counseling stop before severe formal physical punishment. Cold, quiet, and completely uncompromising.
